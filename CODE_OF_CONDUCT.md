# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project spaces should be welcoming, respectful, and focused on constructive technical
collaboration. Expected behavior includes giving actionable feedback, respecting different
viewpoints and experience levels, and accepting responsibility for mistakes.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized attention, publishing another
person's private information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces
where someone represents the project. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
security/contact channel rather than disclosing sensitive details in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior is unsafe, abusive, or persistently disruptive. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical, with attention to both safety and fair review.
